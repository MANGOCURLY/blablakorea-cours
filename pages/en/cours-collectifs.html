Frequently asked questions

Do I need any special equipment? (textbook, software...)

No textbook to buy! All course materials are prepared by me and provided at each session. You simply need Zoom and a stable internet connection.

What happens if no class schedule suits me?

Currently, classes are held on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. If there is enough demand, new slots will be opened at other times. Please indicate your preferred time slot in the contact form; this helps us plan future classes.

For private classes, scheduling is flexible, contact us directly to discuss.

What happens if the group class I registered for does not reach the minimum number of students?

If the minimum number of students is not met or for organizational reasons, you can choose between a full refund or a transfer to another available group.

What happens in case of a technical problem during a class?

If the problem is on my side (instructor), the credit is carried over and a new date is offered within 48 hours. If the problem is on your side, the first time is considered an exception (credit carried over), but from the second time onwards, the credit is lost.

How many students are there in a group class?

Groups are limited to 3-6 people. In my opinion (Jonghyuk), this is the ideal size to allow for real interactions while maintaining personalized support for everyone.

I am a complete beginner, can I join a group class?

Yes, absolutely! Beginners often thrive in group classes. Before registration, a free level test helps determine your exact level, so you can be placed with students of the same level. And if needed, an adjustment to another group is always possible!

How does the level test work? Is it mandatory?

Yes, it is mandatory for group classes. If the skill gap within a group is too wide, the learning pace will not suit anyone. The online test takes about 10 minutes, and your result (level A0 to B2) will be sent to you by email.


For private classes, a free 30-minute discovery session allows us to assess your level and create a customized program and schedule!

The group level doesn't suit me after the first class, what should I do?

If you notify us within 48 hours of the first class, we can look into changing you to another available group. No additional fee is due if you switch to a group at the same price.

What happens if I miss a group class?

Group classes follow a fixed schedule, so an absence for personal reasons cannot result in an individual make-up session or a refund. The course materials and homework for the missed session will still be sent to you, and you can catch up on the content on Discord.
