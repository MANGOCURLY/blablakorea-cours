Blabla Korea · Korean classes

Cancellation & Refund Policy

This policy applies to all Blabla Korea Korean classes: private classes, group classes and thematic workshops.

A.Rescheduling & absences
24h+ notice

Rescheduling request made more than 24h in advance

Session credit automatically carried over. Usable within the pack's validity period (6 months from purchase).

E.g.: class scheduled Wednesday at 4pm → cancellation/rescheduling request possible until Tuesday at 4pm

Less than 24h / No-show

Cancellation less than 24h before, or absence

Credit automatically forfeited. No refund.

E.g.: class Wednesday at 4pm → cancellation message Wednesday at 9am → session forfeited

Group class

Absence from a group class

Group classes follow a fixed schedule. If the learner is absent for personal reasons, the missed session cannot be made up individually, refunded, or carried over as credit.

Unless a commercial exception is granted by Blabla Korea

🎫 No-show Joker

Private class 5 & 10 sessions · Group classes PACK 8

1 no-show joker per pack. Renewed with each new purchase.Non-cumulative
Usable only within the validity period of the current pack. Non-transferable.

No joker: Korean Alphabet workshop, Group classes PACK 4, Private class (single session)

B.Partial pack refund
Partial refund

Refund request after classes have started

Sessions already attended are not refunded. Only the remaining sessions are refunded, based on the price actually paid, after deduction of a 15% administrative fee.

Formula: Remaining sessions × (total paid ÷ number of sessions) × 0.85
Basis: calculated on the price actually paid

E.g. — Group classes PACK 4 at €59, 2 sessions attended:
Price/session: €59 ÷ 4 = €14.75
→ 2 remaining × €14.75 × 0.85 = €25.08 refunded
C.Cancellation after payment
Before start — within 14 days

No test, call, group placement or personalised preparation started

Full refund. No fees.

After start · before classes

Test, call, placement or preparation completed / no session attended

If no class has been attended yet, only a €20 setup fee is retained.
This fee covers the level test, the discovery call, group placement and personalised lesson preparation.

Formula: amount actually paid − €20

E.g. — Group classes PACK 4 at €59, cancellation before the 1st class:
€59 − €20 = €39 refunded

Korean Alphabet workshop: as it requires neither a level test nor group placement, the setup fee does not apply — full refund before the session.

After 1st class

Cancellation after at least 1 session attended

Sessions already attended are not refunded. The remaining sessions are refunded according to the partial refund formula in Section B, with a 15% administrative fee deducted.

Formula: remaining sessions × (total paid ÷ number of sessions) × 0.85

E.g. — Group classes PACK 4 at €59, 1 session attended:
3 remaining × €14.75 × 0.85 = €37.61 refunded
D.Cancellation by the instructor
Instructor's reasons

Class cancelled by the instructor

A new date is offered to the learner within 24h. If no reasonable replacement date can be found, the learner may choose between carrying over the credit or a refund of the session concerned.

Permanent closure

Permanent discontinuation of the service

Full refund of all remaining credits, with no fees.

E.Opening of group classes
Min. enrolment

Insufficient enrolment for a group class

In the event of insufficient enrolment or organisational constraints, Blabla Korea may postpone or cancel the opening of a group class. The learner may choose between a full refund or a transfer to another available group.

F.Pack validity
Pack expiry

Valid for 6 months from purchase

Reminder email sent 4 weeks before expiry. Unused credits expire automatically.

Valid until 11:59pm on the day falling 6 months after purchase (Europe/Paris time)

G.Level mismatch
Level adjustment

Unsuitable level after the 1st session

A group change request may be reviewed within 48h of the 1st session, subject to available places. No surcharge is due if the new group is at the same price.

The group change is only confirmed after validation by Blabla Korea

H.Technical issues
Instructor's side

Instructor's connection or equipment failure

Credit carried over. New date offered within 48h.

Learner's side

Learner's connection or equipment failure

1st time: exception granted, credit carried over.
From the 2nd time onwards: credit forfeited.
