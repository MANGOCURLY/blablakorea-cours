Frequently asked questions

Before you start

I am a complete beginner, can I join a group class?

Yes, absolutely! Beginners often thrive in group classes. Before registration, a free level test helps determine your exact level, so you can be placed with students of the same level. And if needed, an adjustment to another group is always possible!

How does the level test work? Is it mandatory?

For group classes, yes, it is mandatory. If the level difference within a group is too significant, the learning pace will no longer suit anyone. The test is done online in about 10 minutes, and the result (level A0 to B2) is sent to you by email.


For private classes, a free 30-minute discovery session allows us to assess your level and create a customized program and schedule!

Is there a free trial / discovery session?

Yes! For private classes, a free 30-minute discovery session is offered. For group classes, a level test or a brief discussion (by message, or video call if necessary) helps us determine the best approach together before you start!

Course format

How are the classes conducted? (Zoom? Recorded?)

Classes are held live on Zoom (or Google Meet). For copyright and confidentiality reasons, classes are not recorded, but a summary of what was covered is sent after each session for easy review.

Please note that we may take a screenshot during the class to verify that all participants are present.

How many students are there in a group class?

Groups are limited to 3-6 people. In my opinion, this is the ideal size to allow for genuine interactions while maintaining personalized support for everyone.

Are there homework assignments or follow-up after classes?

Yes, after each class you receive a summary of the expressions covered + homework assignments. On Discord, you can continue to practice daily through pronunciation challenges, exercises, or by asking your questions. I personally correct and interact on every homework assignment and every Discord challenge!

Practical information

Do I need any special equipment? (textbook, software...)

No textbook to buy! All course materials are prepared by me and provided at each session. You simply need Zoom and a stable internet connection.

What if no time slot suits me? (group class)

Currently, group classes are held on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. If there is enough demand, new slots will be opened at other times. Please indicate your preferred time slot in the contact form; this helps us plan the various classes.

For private classes, scheduling is flexible. Contact us directly to discuss.


Do the packages have an expiration date?

Yes, 6 months from the purchase date. A reminder email is sent 4 weeks before expiration, so you can use your remaining sessions comfortably.

What happens if the group class I registered for does not reach the minimum number of students?

If the minimum number of students is not met or for organizational reasons, you can choose between a full refund or a transfer to another available group.

About us

What makes Blabla Korea different from other online Korean schools?

After three years of teaching both French- and English-speaking students, I realized that the real problem isn't knowing grammar, but not being able to speak. That's why at Blabla Korea, we focus on speaking practice—starting from the very first lesson.


Blabla Korea's greatest asset is learning to use the language through role-playing and games, not just through memorized expressions or rules.

Cancellation · Make-up classes · Refund

What happens if I can't attend a class? (private classes)

If you give at least 24 hours' notice, the session credit is automatically carried over and remains freely usable throughout the package's validity period (6 months after purchase). However, a cancellation less than 24 hours in advance or a no-show results in the loss of the credit. For Boost Packs (5 sessions), 8 Weeks (8 sessions), and Immersion (10 sessions), one no-show joker is offered per pack.

What happens if I miss a group class?

Group classes follow a fixed schedule, so an absence for personal reasons cannot result in an individual make-up session or a refund. The course materials and homework for the missed session will still be sent to you, and you can catch up on the content on Discord.

Can I get a refund after paying?

Yes, it depends on the situation:


  • Within 14 days of purchase & before classes/follow-up begin: full refund

  • Level test or call already completed, but no classes taken: refund of the amount paid, minus €20 initialization fees

  • After at least 1 class attended: sessions already completed are not refunded; remaining sessions are refunded with a 15% deduction for administrative fees


The details of calculations and examples are available in the [Full cancellation and refund policy].

The group level doesn't suit me after the first class, what should I do?

If you notify us within 48 hours of the first class, we can look into changing you to another available group. No additional fee is due if you switch to a group at the same price.

What happens in case of a technical problem during a class?

If the problem is on my side (instructor), the credit is carried over and a new date is offered within 48 hours. If the problem is on your side, the first time is considered an exception (credit carried over), but from the second time onwards, the credit is lost.
