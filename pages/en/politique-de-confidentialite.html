Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026

  1. About Us
    Blabla Korea a France-Korea facilitation service provided by Jeanne Lethielleux and Jonghyuk Lee. If you have any questions regarding your personal data, please contact us at: contact.blablakorea@gmail.com

  2. What data do we collect?
    We only collect the data you voluntarily provide us through our contact form: first name, last name, email address, and the content of your message. We do not collect any browsing data, do not use analytical cookies, and do not use any tracking tools.

  3. Why do we use this data?
    Your data is used exclusively to respond to your request and to discuss your project with you. It is never shared with third parties, resold, or used for commercial purposes.

  4. How long do we keep your data?
    Your data is kept for the time necessary to process your request, and for a maximum of 3 years from our last exchange.
